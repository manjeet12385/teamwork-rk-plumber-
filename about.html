About us

Practical plumbing, finished with care

Team RK Plumber is a local plumbing and bathroom-fitting service based in Lormi. We help homeowners and project teams with new-home piping, bathroom installations, kitchen plumbing, and everyday repairs.

Every project starts with understanding what you need and where the work is required. We discuss the scope with you, explain the next steps, and focus on neat, functional fittings suited to your space—from essential pipework to the finishing details in your bathroom.
